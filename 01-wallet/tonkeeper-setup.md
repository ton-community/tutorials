# How to Set Up Tonkeeper and Buy Your First TON

Welcome to the TON ecosystem! Tonkeeper is the most popular and user-friendly non-custodial wallet for the TON blockchain. This guide will walk you through the process of setting up your wallet and acquiring your first TON coins from scratch.

---

## 🛡️ Step 1: Installation

1. **Download the App**: Search for "Tonkeeper" in the [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/tonkeeper/id1551532000) or [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.tonkeeper).
2. **Verify the Developer**: Ensure the app is published by **Tonkeeper**. Be cautious of fake apps.
3. **Launch**: Open the app and select **"Create a new wallet"**.

---

## 🔑 Step 2: Creating Your Wallet & Security

Since Tonkeeper is **non-custodial**, you are the only one who holds the keys to your funds.

1. **Backup Your Recovery Phrase**: The app will generate a 24-word recovery phrase (seed phrase). 
   - ⚠️ **CRITICAL**: Write these words down on a physical piece of paper.
   - ⚠️ **NEVER** share this phrase with anyone.
   - ⚠️ **NEVER** save it in a screenshot, email, or cloud storage.
2. **Confirm the Phrase**: The app will ask you to verify the phrase to ensure you've saved it correctly.
3. **Set a Passcode**: Create a secure PIN or enable Biometrics (FaceID/TouchID) for quick and safe access.

---

## 💰 Step 3: How to Buy TON

There are three primary ways to get TON into your wallet:

### Option A: Buy Directly in Tonkeeper (Fastest)
Tonkeeper integrates third-party payment providers (like MoonPay or Banxa) to allow you to buy TON with a credit card.
1. Tap the **"Buy"** button on the main screen.
2. Select your preferred provider.
3. Enter the amount of TON you wish to purchase.
4. Follow the KYC (Know Your Customer) process and complete the payment.

### Option B: Use a Centralized Exchange (CEX)
If you have an account on Binance, OKX, Bybit, or KuCoin:
1. Go to the **"Withdraw"** section for TON.
2. **Copy your Address**: In Tonkeeper, tap your address (at the top) and copy it.
3. **Paste Address**: Paste this address into the exchange's withdrawal field.
4. **Select Network**: Ensure you select the **TON Network**.
5. **Confirm**: Send the funds. They will appear in your wallet in a few minutes.

### Option C: Peer-to-Peer (P2P)
Use platforms like Telegram's `@wallet` or other P2P markets to buy TON from other users.
1. Find a trusted seller.
2. Provide your Tonkeeper address.
3. Confirm payment via the agreed method.

---

## 🚀 Step 4: Your First Transaction

Now that you have TON, let's test it!
1. Tap **"Send"**.
2. Enter a destination address or search for a contact.
3. Enter the amount.
4. Review the transaction fee (gas) and tap **"Send"**.

---

## 💡 Pro Tips & Security Checklist

- ✅ **Small Test First**: When sending a large amount from an exchange, always send a small "test" amount first to verify the address.
- ✅ **Check the Address**: Always double-check the first and last 4 characters of the address before confirming.
- ✅ **Avoid "Support" DMs**: Real TON support will **never** DM you first or ask for your recovery phrase.
- ✅ **Update the App**: Keep Tonkeeper updated to the latest version to ensure you have the latest security patches.

**Happy exploring the TON blockchain!** 💎
